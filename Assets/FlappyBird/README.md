# Flappy Bird framework

Open `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity` and press **Play**. The game creates its bird, pipes, camera setup, ground, and UI automatically; no art or prefab setup is needed.

- The bird starts perched in a nest instead of a start dialog. The nest scrolls away after takeoff and returns on reset.
- **Space, left click, or touch:** jump from the nest and flap.
- After a collision, the visible game freezes for one second. Then press Space, click, or tap to jump from the collision position and continue with the same pipes and score. The bird can pass through the obstacle during recovery until it is clear, preventing an immediate repeat collision.
- Passing a whole pipe pair awards one point. Best score persists locally.
- A dark storm cloud follows behind the bird in The Gale, with rain and occasional lightning. It keeps a safe distance and cannot damage the bird.
- **No game over** ("And never stops – at all"): crashes freeze the bird for a second and count toward the score, which is the number of crashes. The record (fewest crashes in a full run) is saved locally.
- **Hope:** drains every second (`energyDrain`, more each level) and by `crashCost` per crash. Hearts (`HopeHeart`) appear with every `heartEvery`th pipe, hugging a cap or off the line between gaps, and restore `heartValue`. At zero the bird keeps flying but flaps 20% weaker until it finds a heart.
- **Storm surge:** over the Gale's last stretch (`StormSurge`), rain and clouds thicken, lightning strikes, gusts push the bird up or down, the storm cloud swells and pipes speed up.
- **Warmth:** after the Gale's final pipe, a nine-second sunbreak highlights "That kept so many warm -" over a village with glowing windows. `WarmthScene` draws the new art on fixed pixel grids with point filtering. Pipes fade away, the bird glides automatically, and hope does not drain. Space or click continues after the four-second clearing; the third stanza then blends into Extremity. This interlude adds no pipes or extra level to the three-level run.
- **Pipes** are procedural: each gap is placed relative to the last, and speed, gap size and the jumps between gaps all grow through each level.
- **The nest's tree** is one big tree growing out of the ground (`PixelArt.Tree`, with its crown as a separate layer so the storm can rock it), matched to the world: a summer tree dropping leaves (Hope), a storm-bent tree that sways and sheds torn leaves (The Gale), and a frozen tree dropping snow (Extremity). See `TreeLeaves`.
- **The boss** (`DickinsonBoss`): after Extremity, Emily Dickinson. A coroutine script runs her lines (typed out with a voice blip; Space speeds them up) and her attacks: Fascicles (page gates whose gaps open and shut on `DickinsonMusic.SongTime`), Blanks (`BlankLines`: three word windows, the wrong two inked shut as the bird nears), the Wave (hold with `FlappyBirdGame.FlapHeld` to climb at 45 degrees through a zigzag tunnel), Quills (beams aimed at the bird) and a final medley ending on "and never stops - at all". Between attacks the page turns (`Turn`): an ink page sweeps across with the next attack's name while the bird is set back in the middle; Waves take a `WaveStyle` (width, speed, run lengths, flat stretches). Everything is simulated in world units and drawn in the HUD. Hits call `FlappyBirdGame.Wound` (a crash plus a little hope) and give a moment of safety; she refills some hope while she talks. The fight ends with her asleep, the bird perched on her shoulder, dawn, and end credits (`Credits`; Space skips).
- **Sound** (`SoundFx`): point, crash, heart, thunder, gusts, a note per cutscene word, clicks and the boss's sounds, all synthesized at startup. `DickinsonMusic` adds the boss theme.
- **Settings** (the gear button): a volume slider (**M** mutes), **easy mode** (`EasyMode`: gates that never fully shut, slower beams, wider and slower Waves, half the hope lost per hit) and **Credits** (`Credits`, the same roll as after the boss). Settings, the poem and the credits are pages over the paused game (`Overlay`). All settings are saved in PlayerPrefs.
- **Cutscenes** (`StanzaRibbon`): the stanza streams in as a wave of words the bird flies through; caught words gather into the stanza at the top. Space skips. Meanwhile leftover pipes fade, the backdrop, ground, sky, particles and music cross-fade, and `WeatherFx` rolls the next world's weather in (storm clouds and rain for the Gale, snow for Extremity).

## Customize

For saved Inspector settings, create an empty GameObject in the scene and add **Flappy Bird Game** before entering Play mode. The automatic bootstrap detects it and does not create a second game. Adjust gravity, flap speed, pipe speed, spawn interval, gap, width, and arena height. Keep the camera aspect ratio fixed during a run; restart Play mode after changing the Game view aspect ratio.

`BirdController` handles physics and collisions. `PipePair` handles movement, scoring, and cleanup. `FlappyBirdGame` handles input, spawning, state, restart, placeholder visuals, and UI. To use another scene, add the game component explicitly.

The bird, pipes, ground and backgrounds use the pixel art in `Assets/Resources/DickinsonSprites`, loaded by `PixelArt`; if a file is missing, the game falls back to its placeholder blocks. The levels follow the poem's stanzas: Hope, The Gale, Extremity. See [the art guide](../../Art/README.md) to change or regenerate the art. The HUD is drawn with `PixelUi` (pixel font, 9-slice frames, icons): a level card with progress pips, the score, an energy bar, a poem button, level banners, and small credits in the bottom-right corner. Input supports the project's new Input System, with a compile-time fallback for projects using the legacy input manager. No package changes are required.
